What B54 changes, in 10 lines
  • Scan, ONVIF first: a device that answered WS-Discovery gets one GetProfiles request with no login. A login prompt gives a "needs password" card, and the host gets no RTSP walk.
  • Scan, brand paths stop the walk: when the brand is known, its paths go first, and the first 401 stops the walk. A 200 gives a ready card.
  • Scan, full walk: only for a camera with no ONVIF and no brand match. It also stops at the first 401.
  • Removed: the "5 answers of 401 with the same realm" early stop, and the ONVIF pass's identity page, RTSP check and walk on port 554.
  • Password entry, the 0/1/2 rule: 2 streams from ONVIF: stop. 1: check the brand paths, no full walk. 0 after ONVIF and the brand paths: full walk. Any check stops at 2 streams.
  • DVR: each channel gets its main and sub-stream checked (2 per channel). Deep Re-Probe: one full walk of every path, on request.
  • Other ports: once ONVIF or a brand path has identified the camera, its other ports get no probes (today about 44 requests per extra web port).
  • Layer 2: out of the scan; it stays for password entry and Deep Re-Probe. Layer 1 reopens one connection on Connection: close, reads replies strictly and sends a User-Agent.
  • Login rule: stop at the first login the camera rejects, for every brand. After one success, a later 401 skips that path (at most 2). The card says "Password rejected or camera locked".
  • Brand fixes: the Hikvision NVR entry gets skip_layer2; the Axis query retry runs, as its own field.
