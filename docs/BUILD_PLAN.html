Investigate HDMI-CEC (Consumer Electronics Control)

Requested 26 Oct 2026 in 4.1.0 by CrystalHeeler

Problem
CrystalHeeler's aim: motion on chosen cameras interrupts what any TV in the house shows and plays that camera. Research of 26 Oct (docs/CEC-and-TV-Research.md and .pdf, 52 sources): HDMI-CEC only wakes a TV and switches its input; the picture must come from the device on that cable, so it reaches one TV cabled to the Pi, and the HAOS Pi would also have to draw the camera on its HDMI port
Solution(s)
  • For any TV: an overlay app on Android TV, Google TV or Fire TV (TvOverlay, PiPup fork) shows the live camera over the program; Google Cast or Roku full screen as a second choice.
  • AnyCam decides when (per camera: targets, mode, duration, cooldown, quiet hours) and asks Home Assistant to show it, with go2rtc stream URLs.
  • Network control in place of CEC: CEC is a wire in the HDMI cable and cannot go over TCP/IP, but each brand has its own network control in Home Assistant (Android TV Remote or ADB, LG webOS, Samsung, Sony Bravia, Roku, Philips): power on (if the TV keeps its network on in standby, or Wake-on-LAN), input, apps. A Chromecast or Google TV stick on any TV sends the CEC commands itself when a camera is cast to it.
  • CEC last, only for a TV cabled to the Pi.
  • Questions for CrystalHeeler: which TVs (brand and model, Android or not, any Chromecast or Fire TV stick), overlay or full screen

Home Assistant cannot update one rc to the next

Discovered 25 Oct 2026 in 3.9.0-rc2.0 running on a store install

Problem
The update dialog shows "Up-to-date" and a grey Update button for 3.9.0-rc1.0 → 3.9.0-rc2.0, while the add-on page shows "Update available" (also 3.7.0-rc1.0 → rc2.0, 5 Oct). Home Assistant compares versions with AwesomeVersion, which cannot read "rc2.0" (the ".0" after the rc number), so rc2.0 is not newer than rc1.0. The Supervisor only checks that the versions differ, so its command updates
Solution(s)
  • Now: update with the Supervisor command (Terminal & SSH add-on).
  • Release the next build as the final 3.9.0, or with a higher base (3.9.1-rc1.0); both read as newer.
  • Or change the rc format to "rcN" (3.9.1-rc1, rc2 …); CrystalHeeler's decision.
  • A release-gate check that the new version is newer by Home Assistant's rule
