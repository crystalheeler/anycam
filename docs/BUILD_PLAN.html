B6Waiting on youPriority

Test the Microseven once it is unlocked, and make AnyCam robust to cameras that lock out

Why
CrystalHeeler, 2026-09-29: the Microseven at 10.0.0.22 is recognized and authenticates, but serves MJPEG only, with no RTSP stream. That matches the lockout CLAUDE.md describes, which only a power cycle reliably clears. It may be the same fault as the "Invalid data found" regression open since 2.4.0-rc3.3. It also blocks the last Tier 1 check: that the 2.6.1 ONVIF fix stops AnyCam opening connections inside the Microseven's 5 s cooldown 2026-10-03: the throttle research (docs/anycam_rtsp_throttle_research_report.md) found the connection-rate limit rare: the Hipcam RealServer family (Microseven, Sricam, Vstarcam, older Wansview, Tenvis) is the only documented family, 5 of the 76 entries in CAMERA_DB. The lockout that only a power cycle clears was seen on the Microseven; the research names no other camera with it. The nearest is CVE-2023-50685 in the same firmware family, a crash that recovers in about 45 s
Next step

B11Logs firstPriority

Hardware decode silently falls back to software

Why
Log1: ffmpeg cannot open the Pi's decoder devices (/dev/media0 to 3, "Operation not permitted"), then decodes in software, while AnyCam logs "hw first frame (hevc_drm)". Classic 4K runs at 8 fps; 2.6.0-rc2.5 measured 22 fps. The gray frames CrystalHeeler sees when the classic view bogs down fit this: ffmpeg's HEVC decoder fills any missing frame with mid-gray, and Log1 shows ch7 repeating one identical frame 400 times. Live view does no decoding on the Pi, which is why Chrome was clean
Next step
Parked by CrystalHeeler. When picked up: logs first, then find what changed since rc2.5 (HAOS or Supervisor update, ffmpeg 5.1.8 to 5.1.9, device permissions). Also make the log stop reporting hardware decode when it is not happening 3.7.0-rc1.0 (2026-10-04) adds the diagnostics: the start-up "HW device" lines, /api/diagnostics/hw, and a warning when a picture was decoded in software. Next: CrystalHeeler's 3.7.0-rc1.0 start-up log, then the fix
B27Logs firstPriority

Enhanced View is black for 3 to 10 s after you come back to the page

Why
CrystalHeeler, 2026-10-05, 3.7.0-rc1.0, on a phone and on a computer: with the zone window open, leaving the page or the app and coming back leaves no video for 3 to 10 s. Probable cause (not yet confirmed by a log): the live player (VideoRTC) closes its stream while the page is hidden and opens a new one on return, and the picture comes back only with the new stream's first keyframe
Next step
Logs first (CLAUDE.md rule 2): the add-on log of one leave-and-come-back, and whether it also happens with the zone window closed. Proposed fix, for agreement: on leaving, keep a still of the last picture; on return, show it with a "Reconnecting…" label until the video plays again
